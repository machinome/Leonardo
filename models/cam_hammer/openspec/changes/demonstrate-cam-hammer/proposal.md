## Why

Queue item 010-cam-hammer commissions the gallery's next exhibit: show how
one rotation produces a slow lift and a released hammer stroke. The exact
continuation prompt on 12 September 2026 was: “See the projects/Leonardo.
Build next project.” The gallery commission delegates ordinary design and
the complete local implementation cycle; this is its planning record.

## What Changes

- Add an original, complete timber-framed cam hammer with hand crank,
  cam, pivoted hammer lever, journals and anvil.
- Expose phase/run and Lift, Release, Strike, Run in the real exported viewer.
- Prove the lift contact, pivot capture, strike face and moving clearances;
  retain inspected images, browser checks and historical limits.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `cam-hammer-demonstration`: inspect and operate a mechanically coherent
  rotary-to-intermittent hammer exhibit with honest reconstruction claims.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

New files under models/cam_hammer; gallery registry, queue status and root
README gain the completed exhibit. Existing exhibits receive regression.
Uses installed solid-node, CadQuery and the separate viewer; no dependency
changes. No remote publication. Historical basis: Museum of Science's
[Gadget Anatomy](https://www.mos.org/leonardo/activities/gadget-anatomy.html)
and Venice's [Hammer Cam](https://leonardoavenezia.com/en/), both checked
12 September 2026. Neither supplies our dimensions or establishes a folio.
