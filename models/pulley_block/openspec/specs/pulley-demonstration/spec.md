# Pulley demonstration specification

<!-- Copyright (C) 2026 Luis Henrique Cassis Fagundes. AGPL-3.0-only. -->
## Purpose

Let a visitor trace one continuous rope, measure the three-to-one exchange
of effort distance for load rise, and inspect a bounded educational tackle.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Follow one continuous rope through a three-part tackle

The exhibit SHALL show a supported timber frame, two fixed sheaves, one
moving sheave and block, an attached load and a visible effort end. A visitor
SHALL be able to trace a continuous rope from its secured moving-block dead
end over the first fixed sheave, around the moving sheave, over the second
fixed sheave and to the effort end, with three supporting spans.

#### Scenario: Inspect the reeving

- **WHEN** a visitor examines Low, Mid or High
- **THEN** the route, its attachment points and each sheave's axle are visible
  and the rope does not cross itself or pass through hubs or the stand

### Requirement: Exchange effort distance for load rise

The machine SHALL raise the load one unit for each three units of downward
effort travel, maintaining total rope length including wraps and the dead
end throughout its bounded range. The exhibit SHALL show changing span
lengths and explain this displacement relationship.

#### Scenario: Raise and return

- **WHEN** the visitor moves from Low through Mid to High and back
- **THEN** load and effort move oppositely in a 1:3 displacement ratio, the
  supporting spans shorten together, and the same continuous rope is retained

### Requirement: Inspect and replay a complete moving assembly

The exhibit SHALL offer phase/run controls and Low, Mid, High and Run.
Every advertised combination SHALL stay inside a clear, captured working
envelope and the repeating raise/lower cycle SHALL return without a reset jump.
Every rigid component SHALL remain connected and separate parts SHALL remain
free of positive-volume overlap; the flexible rope SHALL remain routed and
attached throughout the motion.

#### Scenario: Replay and return to a pose

- **WHEN** a visitor runs the machine, inspects a manual phase and returns to
  an earlier setting on desktop or a narrow screen
- **THEN** the same pose returns, the controls remain usable and the model
  continues to satisfy its clearance and attachment promises

### Requirement: Understand reconstruction and physical limits

The exhibit SHALL credit Astra's original single-prompt solid-node design,
link historical sources and gallery source, distinguish the three-part
teaching tackle from the historical 33-pulley study, and state its grounded
fixture and prescribed-motion assumptions. It SHALL disclaim solved tension,
friction, efficiency and load rating.

#### Scenario: Read the explanation

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the exhibit and machine record
- **THEN** historical evidence, original dimensional choices, measured
  verification and unmodeled physical effects are clearly distinguished
