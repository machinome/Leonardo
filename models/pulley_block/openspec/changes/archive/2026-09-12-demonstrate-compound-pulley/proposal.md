Copyright (C) 2026 Luis Henrique Cassis Fagundes. AGPL-3.0-only.

## Why

Queue item 030 commissions a complete three-part tackle that lets a visitor
see why pulling three units of rope raises a load one unit. It extends the
gallery from rigid transmission to a continuous, measured flexible route.

Authority is QUEUE.md#030-pulley-block and the delegated one-prompt workflow.
The exact continuation prompt for this session is:

> See the projects/Leonardo. Build next project.

## What Changes

- Add an original timber-framed tackle, two fixed sheaves, a moving block
  with one sheave and demonstrator load, continuous rope, and effort grip.
- Provide Low, Mid, High and Run, phase/run controls, changing-length labels
  and an explanation of three supporting spans and the 3:1 displacement.
- Deliver independent geometric, rope-length, attachment and clearance
  evidence, inspected renders and a tested interactive export.
- Record the historical sources and educational reconstruction limits;
  complete this machine's OpenSpec and register the finished exhibit.
