From Leonardo's studies

A small window into a larger idea.

The Museo Leonardiano's Tirare composto links its 33-pulley reconstruction to Madrid I, f.36v. This original three-part tackle is a deliberately reduced teaching model of the principle, not a replica of that drawing.

The staggered sheaves, dimensions, frame, pin and socket terminations are Astra's educational choices. Orbit the model to inspect the small return wrap at the moving anchor and the load's separate lifting eye.

What the model demonstrates

Geometry, with its limits visible.

The timber frame is the grounded support fixture. The rope is prescribed as taut, the effort socket represents a secured termination, and the suspended block remains upright. Force balance, tension, sway, knot or crimp strength, friction and efficiency are not solved.

The 3:1 result is a measured displacement relationship. Ideal equal rope tension would suggest a force advantage, but no load rating or lifting capacity is claimed. This exhibit is not a construction plan for rigging.
