## Why

Queue item 040 commissions the sixth machine: a visitor can place a suspended
stone with three independent movements and inspect how screw and rope transmit
them. Authority is the gallery brief and this session's exact prompt:
“See the projects/Leonardo. Build next project.”

## What Changes

- Build original educational CAD with fixed footing, rotating mast and jib,
  engaged lead screw/trolley, hoist drum, sheaves, rope and a suspended stone.
- Provide independent slew, traverse and hoist controls, four placement stages
  and a bounded repeatable demonstration, using the actual solid-node viewer.
- Record historical attribution, reconstruction decisions and mechanical,
  rendering and browser evidence; finish this machine's OpenSpec cycle.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `swivel-crane`: independent, inspectable three-coordinate stone placement.

### Modified Capabilities

None. There are no existing specs in this machine's new planning root.

## Impact

Project-owned `models/swivel_crane/`, root registry, README and gallery metadata.
Existing installed CadQuery, molejo, solid-node and viewer; no dependency edits.
Historical basis: https://museoleonardiano.it/en/opera/swivel-crane/ (accessed
12 September 2026), Codex Atlanticus f.105v. Machinery probably designed by
Brunelleschi, drawn by Leonardo; Boldetti's museum reconstruction dates to 1966.
No museum dimensions or assets are copied. No publication is authorized.
