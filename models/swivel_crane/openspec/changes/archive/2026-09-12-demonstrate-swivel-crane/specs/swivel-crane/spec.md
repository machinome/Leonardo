## Purpose

Let visitors understand independent slew, traverse and hoisting through an
original, inspectable construction-crane study with measured geometric evidence.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Independent stone placement
The exhibit SHALL let a visitor independently slew the jib, traverse its
trolley and hoist a suspended stone throughout the advertised envelope.

#### Scenario: Combined coordinates
- **WHEN** a visitor changes any one coordinate at an intermediate combination
- **THEN** that coordinate moves the actual mechanism without erasing the other
  two coordinates, and the rope and stone clear the complete frame.

### Requirement: Inspectable power transmission
The exhibit SHALL show a mating screw and nut, captured trolley, continuous
rope, hoist sheaves and winding drum connected to the stone.

#### Scenario: Screw traverse
- **WHEN** the screw completes one turn within its available travel
- **THEN** actual trolley travel equals the stated lead and the nut remains
  engaged with positive free play and blockage beyond that play.

#### Scenario: Hoisting
- **WHEN** the stone rises
- **THEN** measured rope take-up agrees with hook displacement, total rope
  length is preserved, and its attached ends and sheave routing remain intact.

### Requirement: Repeatable placement demonstration
The exhibit SHALL provide Pick up, Lift clear, Swing and Place stages and a
short bounded, continuous demonstration with playback and manual controls.

#### Scenario: Revisit a pose
- **WHEN** the visitor runs the demonstration and returns to a previous setting
- **THEN** the same actual geometry pose returns with no accumulated transforms.

### Requirement: Honest public evidence
The exhibit SHALL identify the historical source, Brunelleschi/Leonardo
distinction, original reconstruction choices, grounded footing and prescribed
winding/suspension, without claiming stability certification or lifting capacity.

#### Scenario: Inspect the completed exhibit
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the exhibit on desktop or a narrow screen
- **THEN** the real interactive machine, sources, controls, limits and inspected
  geometric evidence are available with no failed assets or unfinished UI.
