A construction-site study

Recorded by Leonardo.

The Museo Leonardiano record identifies Codex Atlanticus f.105v and machinery probably designed by Brunelleschi, drawn by Leonardo. Luigi Boldetti made the museum's reconstruction in 1966.

This compact CAD is Astra's original educational interpretation. The dimensions, square screw thread, captured rails and trolley-mounted hoist are modern choices. No museum geometry or historical dimensions were copied.

Read the rope

Winding, made visible.

Trace the orange rope from its trolley socket, around the lower sheave, over the upper guide and onto the drum. Its end follows a rotating socket. The drum stores less than one turn: 90° at the bottom, about 249° at full lift.

Winding and upright suspension are prescribed. The footing is bolted to an ideal support; mast and trolley positions are held. The stone remains suspended at both named endpoints. Tension, sway, friction, brake torque, structural stability, termination strength and lifting capacity are not simulated.
