One drive · Two alternating picks

Follow a thread.

Start the viewer's timeline to weave. Orbit the model to see the eccentric shaft below the two heddle frames. Use the named instructions to hold each stage.

  1. Open shed · 60°
    Alternate warp threads separate. The captured eccentric lifts one heddle frame and lowers the other.
  2. Handoff · 90°
    The two arms meet inside the open shed. Their interleaved fingers support the red weft spool.
  3. Beat right · 180°
    The spool clears the ribbon. The reed advances toward the cloth edge to illustrate packing the pick.
  4. Return pick · 270°
    The shed reverses. The spool returns through the opposite opening, creating the next over-and-under pass.
