## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete historical power path
The reconstruction SHALL account for every functional toothed wheel visible
in the reference and connect the input mechanically to shedding, the paired
carrier arms, gripping, beat-up and the lower winding apparatus.

#### Scenario: Inspect a transmitting pair
- **WHEN** a transmitting pair is inspected through its engagement interval
- **THEN** its surfaces clear within declared running play, block independent
  movement beyond that play, and remain connected to supported shafts or guides.

### Requirement: Intermittent historical gear train
The upper drive SHALL include the one-tooth input, six-tooth intermediate and
twelve-tooth output identified by Atlanticus 985r, with mechanically established
indexing and dwell.

#### Scenario: One input revolution
- **WHEN** the input completes a revolution
- **THEN** the intermediate advances one sixth of a revolution, the output
  advances one twelfth, and both outputs dwell outside engagement.

### Requirement: Supported and coupled winding
Both ends of the material path SHALL reach supported winding beams whose
motion follows the reconstructed lower transmission and whose connecting
belts or cords remain seated and attached.

#### Scenario: Feed across the demonstrated envelope
- **WHEN** the demonstrated weave advances
- **THEN** cloth take-up and warp supply follow their measured driving contacts,
  flexible routes remain coherent, and a replay reset is identified as a reset.

### Requirement: Retained spool transfer
The paired arms SHALL grip and retain the spool during insertion and transfer,
with modeled actuation and a documented spring/contact assumption.

#### Scenario: Either handoff
- **WHEN** the spool changes arms
- **THEN** the receiving support and grip engage before the departing grip
  releases, the arms clear one another, and the spool remains clear of the warp.

### Requirement: Traceable reconstruction
The record SHALL distinguish observations of the Vinci object, corroboration
from other objects, manuscript evidence and original completion choices.

#### Scenario: Review a hidden fitting
- **WHEN** a hidden joint, tooth profile or transmission is reconstructed
- **THEN** its evidence and remaining uncertainty are recorded, and an unresolved
  mechanism is not certified as a completed historical reconstruction.
