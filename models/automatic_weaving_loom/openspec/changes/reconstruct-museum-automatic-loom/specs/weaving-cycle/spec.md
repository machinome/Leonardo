## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Accompanied weft carrier
Two mechanically driven arms SHALL accompany and retain the spool from either
edge toward the center and exchange it, following the narrow-ribbon mechanism
described by the museum.

#### Scenario: Transfer across the ribbon
- **WHEN** a two-pick cycle is traversed
- **THEN** the spool travels beyond both selvedges and returns, with measured
  arm/transmission closure and retained support at both handoffs.

### Requirement: Beat-up follows insertion
The reed SHALL approach the cloth fell with the carrier outside the warp and
retreat during insertion, driven by a physically connected transmission.

#### Scenario: Beat and retreat
- **WHEN** a pick has cleared the warp
- **THEN** the modeled drive advances the reed to its forward limit.
- **WHEN** the spool crosses the shed
- **THEN** the same transmission holds the reed withdrawn and clear.

### Requirement: Connected shedding drive
The input SHALL mechanically drive the two heddle frames in opposition,
with captured joints or continuously attached tensioned connections.

#### Scenario: Positive follower engagement
- **WHEN** the cycle is sampled through its lifting and return intervals
- **THEN** the drive stays connected without shared rigid volume, preserves
  declared running play and drives the measured eye positions.

### Requirement: Visible cloth feed
The loom SHALL include lower warp and cloth beams, a continuous visible
material route, alternating warp ends and an interlaced cloth sample; beam
motion SHALL follow the reconstructed winding transmission.

#### Scenario: A complete cycle
- **WHEN** the drive advances through two picks
- **THEN** the feed follows its physical contacts, with material winding and
  any unsolved yarn or cloth behavior described accurately.
