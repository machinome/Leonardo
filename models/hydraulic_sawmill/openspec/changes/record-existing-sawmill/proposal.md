## Why

The implemented hydraulic sawmill has design and verification records but
no OpenSpec baseline. The pilot requests an independently maintained
OpenSpec record for each machine while consolidating the gallery.

This is retrospective documentation of original commit `9f4cc67`, dated
12 September 2026. It does not claim that this proposal preceded that code.

## What Changes

- Record the saw stroke, timber feed, controls, rigid integrity and
  historical limitations already demonstrated by the sawmill.
- Revalidate the relocated model and its actual browser export.
- Preserve the original design and validation documents as evidence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `sawmill-cycle`: the observable sawing and indexed-feed demonstration.
- `sawmill-exhibit`: controls, geometric integrity and honest presentation.

### Modified Capabilities

None. No previous sawmill baseline exists.

## Impact

Machine-local OpenSpec and evidence only. The shared gallery registry and
path relocation do not change the mechanical design. No framework, viewer
or other machine behavior is changed by this record.
