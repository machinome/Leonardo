## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete visible transmission
The reconstruction SHALL include the museum's wheel, crank and rod, guided
and retained blade, rocking feed linkage, driving and holding pawls, ratchet
and winding spindle, rope, end pulley and captured timber carriage as
mechanically connected components.

#### Scenario: Follow the working machine
- **WHEN** the wheel is turned through a complete revolution
- **THEN** the connecting eyes remain captured, the rocker reciprocates without
  stretching its tie, the driving pawl advances the ratchet on its working
  stroke, the holding pawl prevents reverse motion, and the rope draws the carriage.

### Requirement: Rope transmits carriage travel
The visible rope SHALL remain attached to its carriage and winding spindle,
follow the end pulley tangentially, and conserve its total modeled length.

#### Scenario: Inspect a complete pass
- **WHEN** the machine traverses the full timber course
- **THEN** the decrease in free rope equals the increase in winding length,
  the pulley turns consistently with rope travel and the rope clears the frame.

### Requirement: Faithful construction and provenance
The reconstruction SHALL reproduce the museum's table, two-post saw tower,
low channel, under-table feed location, saw attachments and carriage supports,
and identify both photographic evidence and unresolved details.

#### Scenario: Compare the model with the museum
- **WHEN** a visitor inspects the whole machine and its reconstruction record
- **THEN** every visible functional system has a modeled counterpart and an
  evidence entry distinguishes observed topology from inferred dimensions or fittings.
