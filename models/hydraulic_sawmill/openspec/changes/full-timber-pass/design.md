# Full-course reconstruction choices

Authority: the pilot's two requests in this session. Ordinary dimensions,
presentation, verification and local commits are delegated by project AGENTS.md.

Use a 100 mm timber billet, centred initially at X=-65 mm. Keep the 360 mm
carriage and its axles at +/-125 mm, so neither axle crosses the blade. Move
the log cradles to +/-35 mm and give them the same through kerf as the timber.
The front carriage crosspiece remains beyond the blade throughout.

Twenty-seven indexed turns give 124.053640 mm travel at the existing drum
pitch radius, clearing both timber end faces and the blade's full X envelope.
Allow four seconds per wheel turn: 108 seconds per timeline. Declare the loop
duration on the model so live builds and standalone exports agree.

Start winding at 30 degrees and clock the drum cleat accordingly. The final
333.75-degree wrap stays below one full turn; no stacked planar rope is admitted.
Shortening the billet preserves the museum mechanism and avoids inventing a
level-winding system. Dimensions remain educational reconstruction choices.

Group by frame, water drive, saw mechanism, timber carriage and feed mechanism;
subdivide timber and running gear where it helps selection. Keep physical
parts and motion relations intact. Remove drivers/instructions entirely.

Tests independently measure beginning/end clearance, cradle support and slot,
roller containment on rails, attached constant-length rope, full-course
integrity, local tooth transitions, timeline timing and empty input tables.
Inspect endpoint snapshots and both exhibit and native tree in the browser.
