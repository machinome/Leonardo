# Complete timber pass

## Why

The pilot requests the entire timber course, timber that fits the machine,
useful assembly navigation, and timeline-only control (19 September 2026).
The current 350 mm timber starts across the blade and advances just 18.38 mm.

## What Changes

- Size the timber and its cradles for a complete pass within the existing frame.
- Animate 74 wheel turns over 296 seconds, beginning before and ending beyond
  the blade, with continuous attached rope and no overlapping winding.
- Organize the assembly into functional groups and readable part names.
- Remove all instructions and input drivers; retain timeline transport.
- Following the pilot's additional request, fill the uncut kerf with a molejo
  node that recedes as the timber passes the teeth and disappears at completion.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `sawmill-cycle`: full timber travel.
- `sawmill-exhibit`: timeline-only control and assembly navigation.
- `museum-sawmill-mechanisms`: full-course rope transmission.

## Impact

Project-owned CAD, tests, exhibit, timing metadata and documentation only.
No framework or viewer changes. No publication.
