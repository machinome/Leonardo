## Why

The pilot's 20 September 2026 comparison with three Milan museum photographs
identifies a reversed blade, misplaced feed wheel, pushing rather than pulling
pawl, ineffective holding geometry and an incorrectly spaced lower drive.
The existing collision tests do not establish whether a free pawl stays seated.
Subsequent pilot inspection also rejected the low hanging rope attachment,
single end pulley, notched crossmember and outboard wooden support. The
rectangular frame and photographed upper roller/lower shaft arrangement must
be preserved; passing tests of an invented arrangement are not acceptance.

## What Changes

- Reverse the blade and timber course relative to the unchanged water channel;
  place the ratchet on the photographed side of the teeth.
- Reconstruct a pulling hook and a holding pawl whose tooth reaction seats it.
  Derive feed timing from the photographed lever rather than preserve the
  previous, unsupported return-stroke assumption.
- Carry the wheel's round iron shaft into the frame, with a square crank
  connection, layered iron joints, bolts, and the timber supports visible in
  the lower-drive photograph. Remove the remote lateral linkage arrangement.
- Restore the rectangular frame and top-centered carriage haul using the
  photographed upper roller and lower transverse shaft/pulley arrangement.
  Implement the pilot-confirmed two-rope reduction: lower redirecting pulley,
  large driven winding section and thin central hauling section, both inside
  the bed. Wind the first rope inward on the bare ratchet axle and support
  the lower idler on the inward face of the opposite saw-tower upright. Derive
  advance and the longer pass from the transmission, without a speed fudge.
  Keep input winding in one plane close to the gear and stack turns radially,
  as the pilot subsequently specified to prevent a crossing with the rocker;
  account for its growing radius rather than traversing the axle;
  no invented outrigger, drop strap or bypass.
- Prove fits, capture, tooth engagement and self-seating, then inspect rendered
  phases and the actual browser export; revise source and validation records.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `museum-sawmill-mechanisms`: faithful handedness, pulling and holding pawls,
  supported round shaft, square crank drive, layered iron connections,
  rectangular frame and photographed top-centered rope transmission.
- `sawmill-cycle`: feed on the hook's pulling stroke and hold during recovery.

## Impact

Only this machine's CAD, kinematics, tests, exhibit, evidence and OpenSpec.
Retain the complete timber pass, timeline-only control and kerf envelope.
Photographs establish topology, not certified dimensions or load capacity.
No dependency changes, publication, or alterations to other gallery machines.
