## Why

The pilot requests a faithful historical reconstruction of all mechanisms in
the Milan museum hydraulic saw, to understand the whole machine. The existing
exhibit omits the physical feed linkage, holding pawl, rope return pulley,
winding and retention details, and depicts a different water supply and frame.

Authority, 12 September 2026: “See projects/Leonardo/hydraulic_saw and see
pictures at https://www.museoscienza.org/en/collections/objects/hydraulic-saw.
there are details that are not there. I want all mechanisms implemented, this
is an historical reconstruction. research and build it faithfully, so we can
understand the whole thing.” This supersedes the earlier educational-only
scope for this exhibit; the other gallery commissions remain independent.

## What Changes

- Reconstruct the museum's complete visible power path: channel, paddlewheel,
  crank, rod, guided and tensioned saw, rocking feed linkage, driving and
  holding pawls, ratchet/drum, routed winding rope, pulley and timber carriage.
- Match the two-post saw tower, four-legged table, low channel, under-table
  feed mechanism and captured carriage shown in the museum photographs.
- Derive feed from the linked mechanism, measure its joints and rope route,
  and preserve whole-machine integrity throughout motion.
- Explain construction and operation in the actual interactive exhibit, with
  views of the linkage, ratchet, winding and saw attachments.
- Record primary sources, observed details, dimensional reconstruction choices,
  and uncertain hidden details explicitly. No dimensioned Leonardo drawing is
  claimed; the reference object is the later Uccelli/Siriati museum model.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `museum-sawmill-mechanisms`: complete physically connected historical drive,
  feed, rope transmission and construction details, with evidence provenance.

### Modified Capabilities

- `sawmill-cycle`: feed and stroke follow the reconstructed mechanism's geometry.
- `sawmill-exhibit`: inspect and explain all mechanisms and distinguish observed
  museum construction from choices needed to realize unseen details.

## Impact

The gallery's existing hydraulic_sawmill model, tests, exhibit and its own
records; a focused gallery README correction if needed. No framework, viewer,
other exhibit, shop, publication or remote mutation. CAD remains original
project-owned code; museum images are private research references, not shipped
assets. Fluid forces, material fracture and historical structural capacity
remain outside a geometric/kinematic reconstruction.
