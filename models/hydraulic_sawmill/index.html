About this reconstruction

This is a kinematic exhibit. The water is illustrative, the timber has a pre-cut blade slot, and the ratchet actuation is prescribed. It demonstrates geometry and coordinated movement, without simulating water forces, cutting, friction or torque. The indexed pawl carrier is an exhibit interpretation; a complete historical feed linkage is not reconstructed. Each four-cycle pass resets the carriage.

Built in Python with solid-node and CadQuery; the haul rope is evaluated with molejo. The browser renders the actual exported assembly. Validation record · Standard solid-node viewer
