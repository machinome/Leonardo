# Revolutions

A wheel makes revolutions. A machine meets one of its ancestors: a
reconstruction of Leonardo da Vinci's hydraulic sawmill. Follow the
wheel, crank, saw, ratchet and ropes, then read the source behind both
the reconstruction, its geometry tests and this film.

## Reconstruction and limits

This educational model follows the Milan museum's interpretative hydraulic
saw, IGB-37 (1952–1953), and the project's research into Codex Atlanticus
1078r. It is not a surviving Renaissance machine or a certified replica.
Its dimensions and concealed fittings include documented choices.
Motion is prescribed. Water, cutting and the opening kerf are illustrative;
forces, friction, stresses, winding stability and material removal are not
simulated. The film slows selected movements for explanation.
