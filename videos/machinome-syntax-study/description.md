# Machinome — a machine you can read

A release-film segment study: moving sawmill, full-screen Python, moving
mechanism. The real source's waterwheel, drives and crankshaft words receive
emphasis at their measured narration onsets. Omission markers identify source
not shown. The final two-line reveal is editorial, not a live coding recording.

Leonardo da Vinci Gallery. Vibecoded by Astra under Luis Henrique Cassis

AGPL-3.0-only. AI-written script and synthetic feminine narration using
ElevenLabs Jessica / Eleven v3. The pilot approved the continuous pronunciation
of Machinome. No music. Optional subtitles are supplied separately as SRT.

The sawmill is an educational geometric/kinematic reconstruction informed by
the Milan museum interpretation of Leonardo's hydraulic saw study. Fluid and
cutting forces are not simulated. The closing view hides the supporting frame
to expose the mechanism. Actual source and export revision:

remain pending. Nothing has been uploaded or published.
