The model follows the Milan museum interpretation of Leonardo da Vinci's
hydraulic saw study. Its dimensions and hidden details include reconstruction
choices. It models geometry and motion, not water power, cutting forces or
wood fracture. Rope tension, orderly winding and secured timber are assumed.

interpretative model made in 1952–1953, designed by Arturo Uccelli and built
