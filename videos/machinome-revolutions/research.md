The [Milan catalogue, IGB-37](https://collezioni-online.museoscienza.org/detail/IT-MUST-NTR001-008357/sega-idraulica)
identifies its object as an interpretative model made in 1952–1953, designed
by Arturo Uccelli and built by Alessandro Siriati. Its account places
Leonardo's study within an older tradition of water-powered saws. The
film's “five hundred years” is approximate Renaissance context; “revolution”
describes changing human work and literal rotation, not a claim about the
historical period named the Industrial Revolution. The film reproduces no
museum photographs or manuscript scans.

The [project's reconstruction record](../../models/hydraulic_sawmill/docs/reconstruction.md)
supplies the detailed mechanism and records the pilot's interventions:
undershot water, a pulling hook, the gear's side, two-rope reduction, winding
placement and fitted joinery. It distinguishes observed arrangement from
chosen internal dimensions. These interventions support the human-direction
sequence without inventing a chat transcript.

The [transmission certificate](../../models/hydraulic_sawmill/docs/transmission-validation.md)
and [current frame certificate](../../models/hydraulic_sawmill/docs/carpentry-validation.md)
document existing geometry, motion and browser checks, with their bounds.
This production does not rerun or extend their physical claims. The model
advances during the downward pulling stroke, holds during recovery, and
conserves the two routed rope lengths geometrically. Tension, slip, fluid
power, cutting loads and wood fracture are not solved.

The [published Machinome package description](https://pypi.org/project/machinome/0.7.0/)
describes a Python framework that brings machine geometry, relationships,
motion and tests into source code. It also records AI-assisted development
from mechanical projects. Those are the product claims used in the film.
Its site and release-status claims are not reused without independently
verifying destinations. This release film intentionally carries no version
number. The manual URL could not be fetched through the web research tool;
it is not treated as a verified public viewing destination for the sawmill.

The two code lines shown in the film are verbatim declarations in
`models/hydraulic_sawmill/model.py` at the pinned export revision. They show
real motion dependencies; they are not presented as a complete sawmill program.

Authorship, copyright and gallery licensing come from the project's README,
NOTICE and LICENSE. The upload description retains the recorded “Astra”
attribution and identifies this film's script as AI-authored and its voice
as synthetic. No separate licence is invented for the generated MP4.
